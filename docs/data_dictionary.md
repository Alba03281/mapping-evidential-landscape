# Data Dictionary

## publication_basic.csv

### pub_id
Unique publication identifier.

### title_full
Full publication title as extracted from the document.

### year_pub
Publication or submission year.

### authors_raw
Author names as they appear in the publication.

### first_author
First listed author.

### institution_raw
Institutional affiliation as reported in the publication.

### source_type
Standardized publication type:
- journal_article
- dissertation
- thesis_master
- excavation_report
- brief_report
- other

### language
Primary document language:
- en
- zh
- mixed
